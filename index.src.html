ChainX limited warranty

Every T5DM and T5EV is covered for 24 months or 100,000 km, whichever comes first, and serviced by our team in Tema.

Cover starts on the day the truck is delivered or first used, whichever comes first, and ends at the time or distance shown, whichever comes first. Parts repaired or replaced under warranty are covered for the rest of the original period.

Taking care of the battery

All lithium batteries lose some capacity with time and use. That gradual loss is normal and is covered only if state of health falls below 70 percent within the period. Battery cover does not apply to damage from:

  • Leaving the battery at zero charge for long periods
  • Physical damage, or any attempt to shorten its life
  • Fire, or immersing the battery in water or other fluids
  • Opening the battery case, or service by anyone other than ChainX or an approved technician
  • Corrosive chemicals, or extreme heat or cold for more than 24 hours
  • Charging with incompatible equipment or the wrong procedure

Not covered

  • Parts that wear with use: tires, brake pads, wiper blades, bulbs, fuses, filters, coolant, refrigerant, oils and fluids
  • Routine servicing and adjustments
  • Accidents, collisions, theft, vandalism, fire or explosion from outside causes
  • Floods, storms, lightning and other natural events
  • Overloading beyond the 4,495 kg gross vehicle weight, racing or other misuse
  • Repairs, changes or parts not made or approved by ChainX
  • Normal noises, vibration, small dents, scratches and paint chips
  • Glass damage, unless caused by a defect
  • Damage from driving on after a fault appears, or reporting it late
  • Trucks with an altered VIN or odometer, or written off by an insurer

Keeping your cover

  • Service the truck at ChainX Tema or an approved workshop on the schedule in your handbook
  • Keep your service records in the ChainX app, including dates and mileage
  • Use genuine parts and the specified refrigerant
  • Follow any recall notice from ChainX or BYD
  • Stop driving and contact us as soon as a fault appears

Making a claim

  • Tell us. Message our service team on WhatsApp with your truck and the fault.
  • We check it. Bring the truck to Tema, or we arrange an inspection, and confirm what’s covered.
  • We fix it. Covered repairs are done at no cost for parts or labor. We repair, or replace with a new or reconditioned part, at our choice.

If you sell the truck, the remaining cover passes to the next owner at no cost.
